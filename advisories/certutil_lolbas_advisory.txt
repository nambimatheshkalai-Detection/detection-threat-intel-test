Threat Advisory: Abuse of Certutil.exe for Payload Delivery
Threat actors are increasingly utilizing Living-off-the-Land Binaries (LOLBAS) to evade defensive measures. Recent campaigns highlight the abuse of the native Windows utility certutil.exe to download remote payloads. Attackers execute certutil.exe using the -urlcache, -split, and -f flags to retrieve malicious executables from external command and control servers and write them to disk. Security teams must ensure coverage against certutil.exe initiating unauthorized external network connections or downloading executable files
