Kiteworks issued an emergency advisory urging its global clients to completely shut down their servers for nine hours following a critical threat warning.Incident OverviewTarget: Kiteworks (A major secure file transfer and data sharing platform used by government, healthcare, and financial sectors)Date: September 25–27, 2026Method: Exploitation prevention against an imminent zero-day vulnerability threatImpact: Global service disruption across critical industries to prevent potential mass data theftWhat HappenedOn September 25, 2026, Kiteworks received high-priority intelligence from federal agencies warning that an advanced threat actor was planning an immediate attack. The threat centered around a zero-day vulnerability in Kiteworks' secure file transfer platform, which allows organizations to move large, highly sensitive datasets.Instead of waiting for a hack to occur, Kiteworks took the rare and aggressive step of advising all customers to take their self-managed systems entirely offline for a coordinated nine-hour window over the weekend. The preventative shutdown concluded on September 27 after the risk window passed, successfully averting what federal authorities feared could have been a catastrophic supply-chain data breach.
