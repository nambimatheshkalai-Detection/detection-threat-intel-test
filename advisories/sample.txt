SIMBA Singapore confirmed a major data breach on September 25, 2026, exposing the personal details of over 23,500 customers.Incident OverviewTarget: SIMBA Singapore (Telecommunications company)Date Discovered: September 24, 2026Impact: 23,549 customers had their sensitive personal information compromisedData Exposed: Names, identity card (IC) numbers, dates of birth, mobile numbers, and email addressesWhat HappenedThe telco discovered the unauthorized access on September 24 and managed to resolve the underlying technical incident within a day. According to statements from SIMBA Singapore, the threat actors managed to exfiltrate full registration profiles of the affected users. Fortunately, the company confirmed that no financial information, credit cards, or bank account details were stored in the compromised directory. The company immediately began notifying affected users and working with cybersecurity regulators to monitor for potential misuse of the stolen identities.
